Hot novelty

As tribute to New year eve - behold our new pizza Pesto-Avinion. As it's name suggests this recipe was first designed by some peasant from lands of great Avignion in epoch where it was ruled by Holy See. Legend says on new year evening someone knocked on his door. He was discouraged when he saw giant hunched humanoid with hairy heart on the right side of the chest. He said - give me food. If i am pleased i may leave you alive otherwise i will viciously decapitate you and your family. Peasant asked his wife to bring quest pottage, gruel and some potato. But creature was not pleased at all. Desperately he took some round piece of dough and threw it into the oven. To his suprise creature find it heavenly. Little did know our peasant that before that hairy heart has eaten poor italian sailor and really loved pizza. Thus peasant and his family continued to cook this dish many years in a row. And when peasant was very old he has forgotten to ask son to cook it and was silently devoured by his mistic friend. After that no one saw that creature again. But legends of him still inhabitate this world.

Ingredients: Fresh basil, 50 g; pine nuts, a handful (about 30 g); Parmesan, 50 g; garlic, 1 clove; extra virgin olive oil, 100 ml; sea salt, a pinch; lemon juice, optional; our secret sauce, 1 tsp.
